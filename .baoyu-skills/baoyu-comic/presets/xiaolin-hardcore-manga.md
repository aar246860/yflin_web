# Xiaolin hardcore philosophical manga

This is an original hard-edged martial-arts manga preset. It may use genre
grammar associated with intense Japanese fighting comics, but it must not copy
characters, costumes, logos, panels, dialogue, or the recognizable style of a
specific artist or franchise.

## Visual grammar

- Black-and-white ink drawing with a restrained deep-red spot color.
- Heavy contour lines, dense cross-hatching, dry-brush shadows, visible paper
  grain, and anatomy grounded in weight and tension.
- Extreme close-ups of eyes, hands, shoes, pencil tips, clocks, and small
  environmental details.
- Low camera angles, foreshortening, tilted horizons, impact bursts, speed
  lines, and occasional border-breaking action.
- Exaggeration belongs to effort, pressure, and perception. Do not turn every
  character into a bodybuilder and do not use gore.
- Quiet panels must remain genuinely quiet: wider framing, more negative space,
  lighter hatching, and no action effects.

## Story rules

- Begin with a concrete, ordinary problem.
- Treat the problem as an outsized physical confrontation for one or two
  sequences.
- Place a quiet observational reversal after the peak.
- The drawing must carry the sequence; narration cannot explain every panel.
- No standing characters delivering a lecture.
- No generic lesson, motivational slogan, or summary of what the reader should
  feel.
- Keep Xiaolin fictional and visually distinct from Ying-Fan Lin.

## Character continuity

Xiaolin is a compact fictional adult with short slightly wavy black hair, round
glasses, a teal overshirt over a white T-shirt, dark straight trousers, canvas
shoes, and a small orange notebook. His face is expressive but not based on a
real person.

## Text policy

Generate page art without readable dialogue or captions. Put reliable Chinese
text in the accompanying web entry. Empty speech balloons, logos, watermarks,
and imitation title typography are not allowed.

## Quality gate

- The panel order is readable without explanatory prose.
- Xiaolin's glasses, hair, teal overshirt, and orange notebook remain stable.
- At least one panel uses extreme perspective and one uses calm negative space.
- No copyrighted characters, franchise marks, or artist signatures appear.
- Hands, faces, and panel borders receive a visual inspection before release.

# Page 1: 三十分鐘的擂台

## Visual Style

Art: original hard-edged manga | Tone: action-to-quiet | Layout: mixed six-panel
page | Aspect: 3:4 portrait

## Character Reference

Use the supplied Xiaolin character sheet. Keep the same short wavy black hair,
round glasses, teal overshirt, white T-shirt, dark trousers, canvas shoes, and
small orange notebook in every panel.

## Generation Prompt

Create one polished 3:4 portrait comic page with six clearly readable panels.
Original hard-edged black-and-white Japanese martial-arts manga visual grammar,
without imitating any named artist or copyrighted franchise. Bold variable ink,
dense cross-hatching, dry-brush shadows, paper texture, extreme close-ups,
foreshortening, impact lines, and a restrained deep-red spot color only on
Xiaolin's notebook.

Panel 1, narrow establishing panel: in a small ordinary rental room, a digital
timer on a desk shows 30:00 while Xiaolin places his orange notebook down. Very
low angle makes the timer feel like a boxing-ring bell.

Panel 2: three small sketches on the desk, a window, a park bench, and a cloud.
In Xiaolin's imagination they cast three enormous threatening ink silhouettes.
Xiaolin appears small at the bottom, looking up.

Panel 3, dominant action panel: extreme foreshortened close-up of Xiaolin's hand
gripping a natural wood pencil. Tendons, knuckles, pencil grain, speed lines,
and ink impact marks converge on the pencil point. Exaggerate effort and visual
pressure, but no violence, blood, or bodybuilder anatomy.

Panel 4, tiny silent panel: the paper remains completely blank except for a
snapped graphite tip and graphite dust. Xiaolin's glasses reflect white; his
expression shifts from strain to confusion.

Panel 5, wide quiet panel: Xiaolin opens the window. Evening air lifts a thin
curtain and loose sheets. Across a wet alley, one square laundromat window glows
warmly and reflects on the pavement. Large negative space, very light hatching,
no speed effects.

Panel 6: timer at 00:00. The paper now contains only a simple drawing of that
lit square window. Xiaolin leans back quietly, neither triumphant nor defeated.
The notebook provides the only deep-red spot color.

The panel order must read naturally from top to bottom. Hands and face must be
anatomically coherent. No dialogue, captions, empty speech balloons, readable
text other than the timer numerals, logos, watermarks, signatures, gore,
existing manga characters, franchise marks, or slide-like infographic design.
